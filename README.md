---

## 🔹 Step 1 – Sign in
1. Go to [https://portal.azure.com](https://portal.azure.com)  
2. Sign in using the **same Microsoft account** you used during the event.  

---

## 🔹 Step 2 – Check your subscription
1. In the search bar at the top, type **Subscriptions**  
2. Click **Subscriptions**  
3. You’ll see your active subscription (example: “Azure for Students” or “Free Trial”)  

---

## 🔹 Step 3 – Delete all resources
1. Click **Resource Groups** from the left-side menu  
2. Open each resource group you created  
3. Click **Delete resource group**  
4. Type the group name to confirm deletion  
✅ This removes all VMs, Databases, Storage Accounts, Web Apps, etc.  

> ⚠️ Important: Make sure you delete all resource groups to stop any usage or billing.  

---

## 🔹 Step 4 – Cancel your subscription
1. Go back to **Subscriptions**  
2. Click on your active subscription  
3. On the top bar, click **Cancel subscription**  
4. Read and confirm the cancellation  

✅ Your Azure subscription is now cancelled, and billing will stop immediately.  

---

## 🔹 Step 5 – Wait for 3 days
After cancellation, Azure keeps your subscription in **“Canceled”** state for about **3 days**.  
During this time, billing is stopped, and the system prepares your subscription for deletion.  

---

## 🔹 Step 6 – Delete the subscription permanently
1. After 3 days, go back to **Subscriptions** again  
2. Click your canceled subscription  
3. Click **Delete subscription**  
4. Confirm the deletion  
✅ This completely removes it from your Azure portal  

---

## 🔹 Step 7 – Remove payment method (if added)
1. Go to **Cost Management + Billing**  
2. Select **Billing profiles → Payment methods**  
3. Choose your card → Click **Detach / Delete**  
> ⚠️ You can remove your card only if there are no unpaid invoices or active subscriptions  

---

## 🔹 Step 8 – Final verification
1. Go to **Subscriptions** → It should show **Canceled** or **Deleted**  
2. Go to **Resource Groups** → It should show **Empty**  
✅ If both are done, your Azure account is fully terminated  

---

## 🔹 Step 9 – (Optional) Close your Microsoft account
If you created a separate account only for this event and no longer need it:
1. Visit [https://account.microsoft.com](https://account.microsoft.com)  
2. Click **Your Info → Close Account**  
3. Follow Microsoft’s process (it will close after 30 days)  

---

## ✅ Done!
🎉 You have successfully terminated your Azure account.  
No charges will occur, and all event-related data is deleted.
